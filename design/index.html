About me

A little bit about who I am

The short version — and the slightly longer one.

I'm Minh An — a web developer and designer based in Ho Chi Minh City.

For the past three years I've helped startups and small teams shape their ideas into products: mapping out what to build, designing the screens, and shipping the code. I care about the details most people never notice — the half-second animation, the error message that explains itself, the button that feels right to click.

When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll find me with a camera in hand, exploring the city's backstreets, or hunting down the best coffee in town.

Case study

Travel Buddy

Product design & front-end · 8 weeks

The problem. Planning a trip means juggling five tabs, two spreadsheets and a group chat nobody reads.

What I did. Interviewed four frequent travellers, mapped the core flow to a single itinerary view, and built a responsive Next.js app with offline-friendly checklists and a shareable link.

The result. A test group of 12 planned a weekend trip in 20 minutes — down from a full evening. The design and code are fully placeholder; replace with your real story.

Bloom Store

Full-stack build · 6 weeks

The problem. A small flower shop's existing store looked great on a laptop and fell apart on a phone — where most of their customers actually were.

What I did. Rebuilt the storefront mobile-first with a persistent cart, honest delivery messaging, and a three-step checkout that removes every field we could.

The result. Mobile conversion up 38% in the first month, and the shop owner finally stopped apologising for the website. The design and code are fully placeholder; replace with your real story.

Pulse Dashboard

Front-end & data visualisation · 5 weeks

The problem. A startup drowning in product events with no way to see what mattered day to day.
